Art Basel Hong Kong 2025

ASIAN GALLERIES REPRESENT 45.8%, SIGNALING SHIFT TO ASIA

2025.3.31 CHEN RUQING, WANG YUNG!

Art Basel Hong Kong 2025 closed on March 30, marking the significant shift in the global art scene. This year, Asian galleries made up 45.8% of all exhibitors, while European and North American numbers slipped.

The 2025 edition of Art Basel Hong Kong concluded on Sunday, March 30, with strong sales reported across all sectors. The fair attracted over 85,000 visitors during its five-day run, with collectors and institutions from across Asia demonstrating particularly strong buying power.

This year's fair featured 242 galleries from 40 countries and territories, with 45.8% of exhibitors hailing from Asia. This marks a significant increase from previous years and underscores the region's growing importance in the global art market.

European galleries accounted for 32.5% of participants, down from 35.2% in 2024, while North American representation fell to 18.3% from 21.1% last year. Galleries from other regions made up the remaining 3.4%.

"The shift toward Asia is undeniable," said fair director. "We're seeing not only more Asian galleries participating, but also stronger engagement from Asian collectors across all price points."
